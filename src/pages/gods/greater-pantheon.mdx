## Asyr Shai

_Greater god of dragonkind_ <Pill zhong/> ⿰日帝（音安） 、祖 <Pill rekh/> <Drac>ASIIR XAI</Drac> 

> [!readaloud serif]
> 混沌安神之暴，震地燃天。金帝之下，皆必顫顛！

Asyr Shai is the mother of all dragonkind, the embodiment of all chaos. Her breath is the wildfire, the beat of her wings the tempest, her roar the thunder, and her footstep the earthquake. She is the most revered amongst dragons.

She created the primordial heaven and the ancient sun and stars, that lashed the earth with rains and scorched it with sun. She created the three dragon races of Heaven, Earth, and Man, to command the domains of the world, as kings of all beasts and the wingless races.

Later, she elevated the venerables of the Stars and Sun to take her place over the material plane.


## Xiron Shai

_Greater god of dragonkind_ <Pill zhong/> ⿰王尨（音羲） 、祖 <Pill rekh/> <Drac>XIRON XAI</Drac>

Xiron Shai is the father of all dragonkind, the embodiment of all order. He carved mountains and rivers from the land, set ore and crystal in stone, and made it all in perfect harmony. He is the second most revered amongst dragons.

For Asyr's world of rain and thunder, Xiron created plants that would grow in the rain, created trees that would sprout after wildfires, and created the beasts to roam the earth. He gave rise to the [[lesser-pantheon#venerable-of-the-moon-god-of-rain|Venerables of the Moon]], that quelled the rains and allowed civilisation to flourish.
