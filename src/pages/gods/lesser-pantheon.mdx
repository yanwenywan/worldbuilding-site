## Golmak, the Fallen One

【中】死神、墮落之神、牧靈者

Golmak was once a greater god, the Shepherd of Departed Souls, god of death. Due to unknown reasons long long ago, he rose up, trying to usurp the place of the Red Goddess, and reap her soul for his own. His armies of the dead rose in the void, and met with the immortal soldiers of the other gods, and their war split heaven and earth, and created a new age.

Golmak fell, not dead, but gravely wounded, and from a shattered portion of the Material World, they fashioned a prison for him, to suppress him forever more in suffering. This became the deep dark, where the darkness carries Golmak's unconsious presence, consuming any unlucky soul to be enshrouded within it.

Wisps of his intent scattered after his fall, giving rise to demonic cultivation, to cults of death, and also corrupting Jin Zhou, turning him into a being of resentment and war.
