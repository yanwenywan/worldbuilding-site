*Those who awoke first, when the world first formed from the chaos; those who awoke later, raised by the elders into positions of power. Those who build it, those who guard it, those who transcend it, those who seek to destroy it.*

## The Greater Pantheon
<Sc>
**the ones who awoke first**
</Sc>

The Old Gods, the ones who awoke before are the deities who created the Celestial Sea, and the ones who still maintain their light touch through the magic that permeates all. They are the most powerful of them all, and each one occupies a realm to him or herself. Their power ordains lesser deities, their will created mortals.

They rarely, if ever, interact with the mortal worlds, and only in the most subtle of ways, whether by answering a prayer with a cryptic riddle, or subtly altering small events, that would not reveal Heaven's design--this is the principle of the old gods, and those who disobey must suffer the fury of their peers.

## The Lesser Pantheon
<Sc>
**the ones who awoke later**
</Sc>

The ones who awoke later are creations of the greater gods. Some were born, others created, yet others still were once mortals ordained and enthroned by a greater god for their own reasons. They take a more present role in the world and are often more the recipient of prayer and offering.

Some even, in rare circumstances, ascend to the position of lesser god through ancient arcane means known only to those who came before.
