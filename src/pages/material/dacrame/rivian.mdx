*Rivenian* 【中】日安王國

*Population: 15 million; Currency: Rivenian Gold Crowns*

The Kingdom of Rivian is the centre of power in the region of Dacrame. Once a great empire that spanned the continent, the kingdom has slowly been in decline ever since, and during the rein of its last dragon Emperor, Caesar Mirmos of House Asarakh. The few marches Rivian has today is a testament to that golden era.

Under the current (human) theocratic dynasty, the kingdom has seen a rejuvenation, and though its recent history has been fraught with war and strife, both civil and external, relative peace has reigned under the stewardship of the House of Andolyn. An uneasy peace, however, with the Kagul horsemen of the great horde having crossed the spirit abyss in the east, to now butt up against Rivenian's western marches.

The kingdom is ruled by Pope Augustin III, who simultaneously is the King (Caesar) James Augustin Andolyn, who has ruled for seventeen years.
