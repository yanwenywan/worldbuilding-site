Traditional homeland of dragonkind, who originated in the western mountains, around the Spring of the Earth.

The lands of Aseron are mysterious and ancient, with a history and a civilisation spanning multiple millenia, back to the first Age, the Age of Fire. Since long ago a tentative trade network has flowed between east and west, from the far east to Dacrame, yet still the two sides know little about each other, only hearing vague tales and stories.

The western mountains cut the continent in two, from north to south. Deep within the mountains, in a region thousands of miles wide, all magic fails, as if consumed by heaven and earth. This is called the _spirit abyss_, and caused magic practioners on either side to evolve different disciplines. Legend goes that this was once a great sea, within which lived a Heavenly Dragon, the _Spirit Eater_, who was corrupted by the Greater God [[lesser-pantheon#golmak-the-fallen|Golmak]] into rising up against Asyr, and was felled by her.

## The Ancient Empire of the Central Plains

<Pill>Demonym</Pill> Yan, Aserian <Pill zhong/> 燕人，中原人 <Pill>Currency</Pill> Silver bullion

> [!readaloud-serif]
> 話說天下大勢，分久必合，合久必分。

The Ancient Empire of Aseron, so it is called by outsiders, is one of if not the oldest continuous civilisations--and unified empires--in recorded history, stretching ten thousand miles from the Damak Valley in the western mountains to the Sea of Shattered Stars in the east.

In antiquity, the central plains were ruled by dozens of small polities, lords surrounding a heavenly king to whom they all swore fealty. These were called the 諸苗 many Miao, and were mostly human kingdoms, or other landed races. Simultaneously, in the western mountains of Damak, Miao culture, philosophy, and Daoist cultivation filtered into the societies of dragonkind, and the dragon tribes eventually came together to form a kingdom of their own, with a heavenly king in the mountains.

That title was eventually won by the Dukes of Yan Mountain 燕山公, and so it was named the Kingdom of Yan 燕國. Yan coexisted uneasily with the polities of the central plains for centuries, before the mountain throne passed to the ambitious and brilliant Yan Xin 燕昕, King Qin 欽王. When he broke through to Transforming Force, the pinnacle of the Daoist path, he lead his dragons east down from the mountains, and the armies of Damak stormed the central plains. The polities of Miao fought hard, some of which, like Mian 澠, even dealing vast damage to the Yan armies in return, but all fell one by one, until all under heaven fell to the Heavenly King in the Mountains, who crowned himself the first emperor of Great Yan.

Not two centuries later, Yan Xin perished trying to ascend to immortality, and his empire, having exhausted itself supporting his grand pursuit, fractured almost immediately. Human lords, sect masters, and dragon nobles all rose up, but eventually were all subuded by the Dukes of Chang 常, who ruled for over three millennia, cementing draconic rule for sixteen thousand years.

### 商 Dynasty of the Great Shang

<Pill>Population</Pill> 219 million <Pill zhong/> 大商、大商王朝

The Human dyansty of Shang, ruled by the Wu clan of Hedong 河東武氏, earls of the city of Shang. 

160 years ago, during a time of great famine, Earl Wu Chang'an 武長諳 found himself leading a rebel army against the dragon Yin 銀 dynasty, with the cultivation sects of the central plains slowly defecting from their old allegiances. Their force was like a tsunami, overruning commandery after commandery, slaughtering the dragon-lords that had oppressed the common people for countless centuries; reaching the capital, they massacred the Yin royal family, and Wu Chang'an would fashion a robe out of the Empress Dowager's hide. The remaining dragon-lords retreated to Damak, unable to fend against the cultivation sects of Shang, and proclaimed a rival dynasty of their own, searching for a time to take back their place as rulers of the central plains.

160 years and five emperors later, Wu Yixian 武羲獻, the aging Emperor Zulong 祖龍帝 has reigned for nearly forty years. He no longer wears the robe of dragonhide, and has even brought back some of the old aesthetics of the dragon dynasties, opening rapprochement with the Zhang dynasty in Damak. Though the two sides still deeply mistrust each other, they live in a begrudiging peace, the two kingdoms' attentions focused more on the Khaganate in Marakar dominating the frozen steppe.

#### 李氏、歐陽氏 The Houses of Li and Ouyang

Though the dragon clans were largely killed or driven out, two notable exceptions remain.

***The Ouyang Clan of Jinshan.*** Leaders of what is largely regarded as one of the most powerful sects on the continent, based around Yunxiao Mountain. Ouyang Jiang 歐陽絳, the late sect master, extinguished a Shang army of a hundred thousand at the foot of Yunxiao Mountain, including all the cultivators of five sects. Though it cost him his life, this struck so much fear into Earl Wu that he granted the Ouyang clan amnesty, and vowed to leave them be, should they hand over their titles as the Marquis of Jinshan, and nominally swear fealty to the Wu like any other cultivation clan. 


The Ouyang sect agreed, and so they persist in Jinshan to this day. The Marquisate of Jinshan was handed to the human Han 韓 family, though the Zhang crown in Khanxanae still recognise the title as belonging to the Ouyangs.

***The Li Clan of Pear Blossom island.*** Pear blossom island is a large island clain several thousand miles off the eastern coast, in the Sea of Shattered Stars. During the dynasty of Li, it was discovered by the old ancestor of the royal house, and he laid roots down for the Li family, once their hold on the central plains falls. 

Li Prefecture was established on the island, and has been controlled ever since by the dragon house of that name. A formation of a hundred thousand pear blossom trees was cultivated there, that could seal off this slice of heaven from the outer world in times of conflict. Even as the Shang dynasty was proclaimed in Longjing, Li remained untouched, and the Shang Emperors never tried to invade across the water. 

Today Li swears fealty to the human dynasty in Longjing, simply because it needs to trade with the continent. Internally, they largely keep to themselves, but are included on the maps as a Commandery of the Shang.

### 張 Dynasty of House Zhang

<Pill>Population</Pill> 27 million <Pill zhong/> 張氏王朝、大張、川國

Zhang Yujing 張宇靖, Marquis of Sixia 四峽候, brother of the Empress Dowager, was naturally the most appropriate successor after the massacre in the palace. He was already the most powerful commander amongst the dragon clans, having rallied the majority of the empire's dragon families in the west, as the Shang advanced thorugh their home of Sixia and overran the central plains.

The dragon-lords were pushed into the western mountains, keeping their hold on Aironhaixi (Yunxin), Damak (Taichuan), and most of the western regions. The Shang could not overcome the dragon-lords in their natural homeland, and so an informal stalemate formed. In Damak, in the provincial capital of Khanxanae, the dragon-lords proclaimed a new Emperor and Dynasty of their own, crowning Zhang Yujing as the Xindai Emperor of Zhang 欣代帝. 

Xindai has ruled over the dynasty of House Zhang ever since, and is still healthy and in the prime of life, though neither of his two sons, Zhang Ping 張平 and Zhang Tai 張泰, seem to be interested in governance and rule, and some of the nobles and ministers in court are quietly worried about his succession.

### 金 Jin Kingdom

<Pill>Population</Pill> 7 million <Pill zhong/> 金國

On the Jin Peninsula, with their western exit blocked by the Jinhua mountains, Jin has always been somewhat distant with the imperial core--"the heavens are high and the emperor is far".

During the Wu Shang uprising, the Jilin house 吉林氏 remained loyal to the Yin, and fought off Shang troops--not that the Shang tried particularly hard to take over this backwater--declaring themselves an independent polity.

Decades later, after the Wu clan were able to consolidate their hold over the empire, they secretly aided the house of Siao 蕭 to usurp the throne, causing a three year civil war in the new kingdom, and installing a friendly king on the throne. They have, though, as of yet, not tried to re-absorb the kingdom back into the Empire.

### 南雲 Nanyun Kingdom

<Pill>Population</Pill> 9 million <Pill zhong/> 南雲國

In a jungle valley, surrounded by mountains, Nanyun had the same isolation as Jin, though on the opposite corner of the realm. When news reached Nanyun of the Shang capturing Longjing, the ruling Loi 雷 family were unsure what to do. Their loyalty lay with the fallen dynasty. 

As the Kingdom of Nanyun--then a subordinate kingdom in the Yin--were not ruled by dragons, many officials felt swayed by the desire to join the Shang and break free of the capital, but the King's faction quickly purged them all, and declared their independence from the capital.

They are friendly and allied with the Zhang, though the Kings of Nanyun have been reluctant to join the empire proper. Khanxanae, though, does not see the need to force the issue.

## Major Powers

### Ulan Khaganate

<Pill>Demonym</Pill> Ulan, Kagul <Pill zhong/> 烏蘭人、蘭人 <Pill>Population</Pill> 25 million <Pill>Currency</Pill> Silver bullion <Pill zhong/> 烏蘭汗國、大金汗國、諸蘭汗國、北蘭、北蠻

The border between the temperate continents and Marakar is a vast, expansive steppe. Though interrupted at places by taiga forests or mountains, it is largely open, albeit very harsh terrain, with poor soil not suitable for growing crops. A range of rolling hills and low mountains separate the steppe from the central plains, with only a few open passageways, so the tribes of the north, called the Lan 諸蘭, remained unsubjugated, and constantly came into conflict with the dynasties of the south.

The Yin had many Lan tribe tributaries, but when they collapsed these broke free, leaving only remnant territories under Shang control. Then, one century ago, the Ulan tribe conquered or subdued the many Lan, and proclaimed themselves Khagan in the steppes. 

Though marginally weaker than the Shang, the strength of their cultivation clans is still enough to be feared, and their mortal armies are devastatingly effective, cutting through many northern kingdoms and even taking parts of the Shang realm itself. To the west, though the spirit abyss prevents cultivators from passing the western mountains, the Khagan sent horses and men through the harsh wintry highlands, and created an empire spanning horizontally from the east sea to the borders of Rivien itself.

### Luzan

<Pill>Demonym</Pill> Luzanese <Pill zhong/> 盧桑人、黑桑 <Pill>Population</Pill> 1 million <Pill>Currency</Pill> Silver bullion <Pill zhong/> 盧桑

The Buddhist Kingdom of Luzan deep within the spirit abyss, connected yet apart from the Daoist tradition of the central plains. Their isolation has lead to the development of the Buddhist system, similar yet totally unique from Daoist cultivation. 

For tens of thousands of years an unbroken Buddhist lineage has dominated Luzanese society, creating possibly the most stable magical-religious order in the known world, and one of the most powerful, rivalling even legendary names such as the Divine Lake Mountain, the Ouyang Sect, and the House of Jiang in the East Sea.

Luzan is a kingdom with a king, but true power lies with the Bodhisattva who sits in Nyingqir, and the four Arhats in the four directions. The Arhat of the north and west remain in Luzan, the Arhat of the South is the national teacher of Zi'an, and the Arhat of the East, once sat in the Zhang city of Rishirju, has settled in the capital to further his path.

#### Zi'an

<Pill>Demonym</Pill> (Zi'an) Luzanese <Pill zhong/> 淄安人、淄桑人、赤桑 <Pill>Population</Pill> 2 million <Pill>Currency</Pill> Silver bullion

Zi'an is a subordinate kingdom to Luzan. Their kings are separate, but Zi'an is still ruled by the buddhist sect, specifically overseen by the Arhat of the South.

The Zi'an luzanese are known as the "Red Luzanese", whilst the luzanese of Luzan are known as "Black Luzanese", and they generally share culture and customs, all speaking dialects of the same language.

### Taihou Tribes

<Pill>Demonym</Pill> Taihou <Pill zhong/> 太鵬族 <Pill>Currency</Pill> Silver bullion <Pill zhong/> 太鵬

Taihou people occupy the Taihou peninsula, as well as much of the east sea. They are a warrior culture born between mountains and oceans, who spread out far and maintain independent communities. In Aseron, the Taihou have formed into two main confederations of city states, the Daikyoku in the north and Southern Mon in the south.

#### Daikyoku Taihou

<Pill>Population</Pill> 1.4 million <Pill zhong/> 大曲太鵬

The Daikyoku is the larger of the two confederations, but the somewhat poorer ones. Mountain people, they live amongst rice and millet terraces deep in the fertile taihou mountains, not often setting out to sea. Daikyoku warriors are often sought after as guards by central plains families.

#### Southern Mon Taihou

<Pill>Population</Pill> 1 million <Pill zhong/> 南孟太鵬

The Southern Mon are the smaller, militarily weaker, but wealthier of the two confederations. They are water people, plying ocean trade between the continent and the sea, and are often navigators, sailors... or pirates.

## Minor Powers

### Southern Jungle Tribes

<Pill>Population</Pill> 9 million combined <Pill zhong/> 南蠻部落

The southern jungle begins at Nanyun, but the central plains civilisation ends at Lyujiang pass. Beyond that are jungle tribes, living along the mighty Lyu and Ran rivers, formed into several confederations all competing with each other. The northern border of the jungle tribes ends at Zi'an, where the Red Luzanese have settled the Zi'an valley for millennia. The southern jungle is partially in the spirit abyss, so magic is weaker here, and the cultivation discipline of the central plains has not managed to permeate well.

The southern jungle tribes are **Lao, Ngan Rung, Mung Raan,** and **Li Sur**. **Kashkat Ai** is often counted as well.

In the southern jungles, they worship and venerate the jungle serpent, an ancient god whose spine is said to have formed the western mountains, whose heart created the world spring from which the Ran river flows, and whose maw formed the Nanyun bay, which today is ringed by the states of Nanyun, Kashkat Ai, Lan, and Yuntong Commandery of the Great Shang.

### Kashkat Ai

<Pill>Population</Pill> 2 million <Pill zhong/> 艾國

The Ai are a southern jungle culture located around the Nanyun bay, who has imported much central plains philosophy and governance, and formed a centralised kingom of their own based on legalist and confucian principles. Hence, it is distinct from the tribal confederations due to its centralisation and statecraft.

### Lan

<Pill>Population</Pill> 100 thousand <Pill zhong/> 蘭國、閔蘭

A colonial settlement formed during the Shan dynasty by immigrants primarily from the "Yun" and "Yu" regions, who cling to the tip of a peninsula on the south side of Nanyun bay. Though independent from the central plains empire, they are closely aligned and supported by it, as strategic leverage against the jungle states. 

### Jotunshir

<Pill>Population</Pill> 60 thousand <Pill zhong/> 由敦使爾

The city state of Jotunshir in the western regions, cornered on three sides by great powers. Their position downstream of Alang Rak and west of Fenghuangguan makes them an important trade node, and so far have been able to play off Zhang, Shang, and Ulan against each other, but this stalemate is not likely to last.

### Lyu

<Pill>Population</Pill> 3 million <Pill zhong/> 呂國

Lyu is a dragon polity ruled by the family of the same name, created back in the Great Li dynasty. They have persisted as a semi-independent polity ever since, and never pledged allegiance towards the Shang. The presence of the Lizhou Sword Sect in Maraxai, along with their relative strategic irrelevance, made the Shang armies decide to leave them be, though they did lose the Kingdom of Yue on the coast to the Shang.

Today, over half of remaining Lyu territory has fallen to the Ulan Khaganate, and the rest is barely holding on. Ulan armies surround Maraxai.

### Gor

<Pill>Population</Pill> 90 thousand <Pill zhong/> 郭

Gor is a small northern state based around the source of the Silver river. The country was one of two halves: the mountain basin around Gor city, and the downstream lands that held most of their economy. The latter was lost quickly to the Ulan Khagantate, and Gor hangs by a thread deep in the mountains.

### Chuson

<Pill>Population</Pill> 170 thosuand <Pill zhong/> 楚仙

Chuson, one mountain valley over, is similar to Gor, situated behind rough terrain. Unlike Gor, it has not lost any territory, but with the fall of the Kingdom of Gomhan to the east, and the loss of northern Lyu in the west, they are hemmed in from both sides, and their fall is inevitable.
