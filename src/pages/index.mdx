Caeleste Mare, the "Celesetial Sea", is the name that the people of this world refer to it as. Their planes of existence are nought but an archipelago, on an empty expanse of black ethereal water. Legend goes that this patch of sea gave birth to the greater gods, and the greater gods created the many planes and peoples, and enthroned the lesser gods as ministers to govern the laws of nature.

At its centre is the Material World, the land of dragons and men and dwarves and elves, and all manner of other races and cultures. The world is vast and wild; civilisation holds in fertile river valleys and floodplains, whilst the wilderness is the domain of wild dragons and beasts. Mirroring this are the twilight realm and the deep dark, one a lush twilight domain of elvenkind, the other a prison for fallen demons and gods.

Outside are the realms of the greater gods, and their sub-realms; together floating on an endless ethereal sea, so vast and incomprehensible that even the most powerful could not cross it, together weathering the waves of many hundred thousand years.
