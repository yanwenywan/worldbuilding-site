## 十四大龍氏 Fourteen Great Dragon Houses

【張】在朝四峽張氏，籍張家處，居天京（皇冠橋），家主欣代帝陛下張宇靖。   
**ZHANG** clan of Sixia, originating from Zhangjiachu. The ruling royal family, located in Tianjing (Khanxanae), lead by the HM. the Xindai Emperor Zhang Yujing. 

【馬】太川馬氏，籍天京，居天京，家主川公馬仙亥。    
**MA** clan of Taichuan (Damak), originating from Tianjing. Located in Tianjing, lead by the Duke of Damak Ma Xianhai.

【千雲】雲心千雲氏（前趙），籍永春，居永春，家主雲公千雲道。    
**QIANYUN** (formerly ZHAO) clan of Yunxin, originating from Yongchun (Sikhasi). Located in Yongchun, lead by the Duke of Yunxin Qianyun Dao.

【蘭】靜塢蘭氏，籍靜塢，居靜塢，家主靜山伯蘭麟華。    
LAN clan of Jingwu (Rae'asan), originating from Jingwu. Located in Jingwu, lead by the Countess of Jingshan Lan Linhua.

【東方】江島東方氏，籍江門，居天京，家主江下侯東方天烈。
DONGFANG clan of Jiangdao, originating from Jiangmen. Located in Tianjing, lead by the Earl of Jiangxia Dongfang Tianlie

【金】竹帳金氏，籍金流臺，居清臺，家主竹帳侯金晴。
JIN clan of Zhuzhang, originating from Jinliutai. Located in Qingtai (Takros), led by the Marquis of Zhuzhang, Jin Qing.

【花】昌鵲花氏，籍連陽，居天京，家主連陽公花歡。
HUA clan of Changque, originating from Lianyang. Located in Tianjing, led by the Duchess of Lianyang, Hua Huan.

【玉】玉原玉氏，籍玉港，居天京，家主玉原侯玉近川。
YU clan of Yuyuan, originating from Yugang. Located in Tianjing, led by the Marquis of Yuyuan, Yu Jinchuan.

【奄】北趙奄氏，籍北趙，居天京，家主趙侯奄令寒。
YAN clan of Beizhao, originating from Beizhao. Located in Tianjing, led by the Marquis of Zhao, Yan Linghan.

【泰】炎環泰氏，籍古爨，居璋嶺，家主炎侯泰目禪。
TAI clan of Yanhuan, originating from Gucuan. Located in Zhangling, led by the Marquis of Yan, Tai Muchan.

【求】國地求氏，籍長觀，居天京，家主國中公求千里。
QIU clan of Guodi, originating from Changguan. Located in Tianjing, led by the Duke of Guozhong, Qiu Qianli.

【萬】河西萬氏，籍兩神，居天京，家主河古侯萬之臨。
WAN clan of Hexi, originating from Liangshen. Located in Tianjing, led by the Marquis of Hegu, Wan Zhilin.

【歐陽】金山歐陽氏，籍雲霄，居歐陽宮，家主玄海道人歐陽湛。
OUYANG clan of Jinshan, originating from Yunxiao. Located in Ouyang Hall, _Jinshan Commandery_, led by the Xuanhai Taoist Ouyang Zhan.

【李】梨花島李氏，籍連陽，居梨花塢，家主梨花島伯李遷清。
LI clan of Pear Blossom Island, originating from Lianyang. Located in Lihuawu, _Li Prefecture_, led by the Earl of Pear Blossom Island, Li Qianqing.

### 弱、落、已滅龍氏 Minor, Fallen, or Extinct Draconic Houses

【白】雲山白氏，籍雲山，居雲山。雲心小族，子爵，臣於千雲氏而爲其右。    
BAI clan of Yunshan (Asnan), originating from Yunshan, located in Yunshan. Minor house in Yunxin, rank of Baron, servant and right hand of the Qianyun family.

{fallen} 【秦】火山秦氏，籍永恆火，居天京。銀落時大損精氣，族爲人類仙士所屠，所逃者二，今一又越山西去。    
QIN clan of Huoshan, originating from Yonghenghuo, located in Tianjing. They suffered great losses during the fall of the Yin, and much of the clan were slaughtered by human cultivators. Two members survived, and one has already left westwards past the mountains.

{gone} 【銀】河東銀氏，籍銀橋。前皇室，族人爲武商所弑。    
YIN clan of Hedong, originating from Yinqiao. The former royal dynasty, their clan was exterminated by the Wu Shang.

{gone} 【燕】貴林燕氏，古燕山公、山内大天王、中原燕皇。先王燕。燕始帝薨而帝國敗，其族皆失滅。    
YAN clan of Guilin, the ancient Dukes of Yanshan, Sky King within the Mountains, and Emperor of the Yan dynasty in the Central Plains. When the first emperor of Yan died, his empire fell with him, and his clan were lost or killed.


## 商国八大凡族

【武】商武氏，大皇族，籍商邑，居長觀，家主祖龍皇帝武義獻。    
WU clan of Shang, the Imperial Clan, originating from Shang. Located in Changguan, led by the Zulong Emperor, Wu Yixian.

【韓】金山韓氏，大將家，籍金安，居金安，家主韓昆之（名丘）。    
HAN clan of Jinshan, a Great General's House, originating from Jin'an. Located in Jin'an, led by Han Kunzhi (personal name Qiu).

【乾】江門乾氏，籍南統，居江門，家主乾津翰（名儲）。    
QIAN clan of Jiangmen, originating from Nantong. Located in Jiangmen, led by Qian Jinhan (personal name Chu).

【臧】火山臧氏，籍珠山，居永恆火，家主臧潔夜（名一）。    
ZANG clan of Huoshan, originating from Zhushan. Located in Yonghenghuo, led by Zang Jieye (personal name Yi).

【春】玉海春氏，籍明州，居玉港，家主春越青（名螢）。    
CHUN clan of Yuhai, originating from Mingzhou. Located in Yugang, led by Chun Yueqing (personal name Ying).
 
【南宮】長周南宮氏，籍南平，居長周，家主南宮羨。    
NANGONG clan of Changzhou, originating from Nanping. Located in Changzhou, led by Nangong Xian.

【秋】昌鵲秋氏，籍昌原，居連陽，家主秋龍顯（名旱）。    
QIU clan of Changque, originating from Changyuan. Located in Lianyang, led by Qiu Longxian (personal name Han).

【魏】竹帳魏氏，籍重維，居重維，家主魏天嫌（名謁）。    
WEI clan of Zhuzhang, originating from Zhuzhang. Located in Chongwei, led by Wei Tianxian (personal name Ye).
