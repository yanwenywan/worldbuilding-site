## 介

龍歌真經十轉者：

（炼气）启蒙篇 (Refining spirit) the enlightenment chapter [like teaching a child literacy, not grand revelation, it's the most basic chapter]
- 第一 龙威（威严碾压万物） The first: Dragon's Might (an aura that suppresses all things)
- 第二 真火（腹养真龙火） The second: True fire (true dragonfire burns within)

（筑基）正位篇 (foundation building) Rectifying position Chapter
- 第三 承自然（自然认己为尊，言而人兽顺） The third: Receiving [the authority of] nature (nature regards the self as its lord, one speaks and men and beasts submit)
- 第四 意境（真意成境，自作洞天） The fourth: realm of intent (your true intent emanates, and a world within a world)

（金丹）郡侯篇 (golden core) The Noble Lord chapter
- 第五 率领（指山曲而曲之，指水洞而洞之，无灵之物，则为生灵效从） The fifth: Commander (you call a mountain to bend, and it bends; you call water to split, and it splits; even the inanimate things serve the animate)
- 第六 万象（万化哉龙，天地万物，天上诸宿，心动而成之） The sixth: Myriad signs (O myriad forms can a dragon take! All things under heaven, all constellations of the sky, your heart moves, and you become it!)

（元婴）帝王篇 (nascent soul) Emperor Chapter
- 第七 治法（得天地应，言出法随） The seventh: Making Law (receive the acceptance of heaven and earth, your word becomes law)
- 第八 动风（祖引浪雨暴风，故行祖相） The eighth: shifting wind (The ancestor god brought fierce winds and torrential rains, and you are her form)

（化神）天神篇 (transforming force) the heavenly deity chapter
- 第九 天地拜（天地为己所使，自成天宰）The ninth: Heaven and Earth bows (heaven and earth are yours to command, and you become the lord of the world)
- 第十 灭虚（天地授首，一爪斩虚空）The tenth: Extinguishing Emptiness (heaven and earth offer its head; one claw splits empty space)
